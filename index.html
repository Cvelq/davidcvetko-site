Common questions.

How do the 30 days work?

Day 3 you get your payback map. Day 7 the first pre-registered experiment is live. By day 30 the champion test has run in your own Ads Manager. If no ad has beaten your best running ad's CPA by then, I keep working at no additional charge until one has.

Why structure before creative?

Most Meta volatility is a structure problem, not a creative one. When every product has its own ad set, spend never pools, nothing gathers enough signal to scale, and every new test restarts the account from zero. Fix the structure and the same creative starts working.

What does the first week look like?

Access on day 1. I map where the money leaks: campaign structure, budget flow, which creatives have real signal behind them. Day 3 you see your numbers on one page. Nothing ships as a guess.

Why one operator instead of an agency, and how many clients at once?

You get me on every decision, and you keep everything I build: the payback math, the testing system, the experiment ledger. I cap at five clients total, two test seats at a time, one onboarding per cycle. If a seat is open when you're ready, it's yours. If not, you get the next start date.

I already have an agency or an in-house team.

Then I work beside them. The briefs go to whoever produces your creative, the debrief goes to whoever runs the account, and the experiment ledger is shared. What changes is that every test now ships with a hypothesis and a kill line, so the account learns instead of restarting.

What do you need from me?

Account access on day 1, answers within 48 hours, and someone who can approve. That is the whole list. The first experiment runs on assets you already have.

What does it cost?

It depends on the account, so I tell you the number on the call, after you have seen the guarantee in writing. No pricing pages, no packages.
