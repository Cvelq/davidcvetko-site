The Beat-Your-Best-Ad Guarantee

By day 30: your payback map, the testing system live in your account, and every experiment pre-registered with its result and learning recorded. If no ad has beaten your current best-running ad's CPA by then (the champion test: measured in your own Ads Manager, same time window, minimum spend per ad set by your own numbers), I keep working at no additional charge, uncapped, until one has.

Conditional on account access on day 1, approvals within 48 hours and data access maintained. If a condition slips, the guarantee holds on deliverables only until it is restored. Cold or paused accounts are excluded.

What you keep

Everything. The payback math, the installed testing system, the experiment ledger. Knowledge only moves toward you. You can't unsee your payback math or return an installed testing system, so all sales are final.

After the 30 days

After the 30 days.
